Fix the subscription issue in the existing Flutter project.

### Problem

After a user signs up from the **Android app**, they are redirected to the **Subscription page**.

On the Subscription page:

* All subscription plans are displayed.
* The user cannot subscribe to any plan.
* Below every plan, this message is displayed:

**"This plan is not available in the current store"**

### Required Fix

Investigate and fix the Android in-app subscription/purchase flow so that the available subscription plans can be purchased successfully.

Please check the complete subscription implementation, including:

1. Android in-app purchase configuration.
2. Google Play Billing integration.
3. Product/subscription IDs used in Flutter.
4. Whether the product IDs exactly match the IDs configured in Google Play Console.
5. Product query/loading logic.
6. Store availability checking logic.
7. Subscription product response from Google Play.
8. Purchase initiation logic.
9. Handling of unavailable/invalid products.
10. Android package/application ID configuration.
11. Google Play Console subscription configuration.
12. Testing configuration for Google Play Billing.

### Important

* Do **not** simply hide or remove the message **"This plan is not available in the current store"**.
* Find the actual reason why Google Play is returning the plans as unavailable.
* Do not hardcode a successful subscription response.
* Do not bypass Google Play Billing.
* Keep the existing subscription UI and design unchanged.
* Keep the existing subscription plans and pricing unchanged unless the configuration is incorrect.
* Keep iOS subscription behavior unchanged.
* Do not modify unrelated features.

### Expected Result

After signup on Android:

**Signup → Subscription Page → Plans Load Correctly → User Selects Plan → Google Play Purchase Flow Opens → Subscription Purchase Can Be Completed**

If the plans are still unavailable after checking the Flutter code, clearly identify what must be configured in **Google Play Console** and which product IDs/configuration are required.

Before making changes, inspect the existing subscription implementation and explain the root cause. Then make the minimum required code changes and verify that there are no Flutter/Dart compilation errors.
