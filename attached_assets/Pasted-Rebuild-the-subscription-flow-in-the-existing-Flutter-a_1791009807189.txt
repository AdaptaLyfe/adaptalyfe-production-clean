# PURCHASE FLOW

When the user taps Subscribe:

```text
User taps Subscribe
        ↓
Check product availability
        ↓
Start purchase
        ↓
Show purchase pending/loading
        ↓
Store processes purchase
        ↓
Receive purchase update
        ↓
Verify purchase
        ↓
Send verification to backend if required
        ↓
Update entitlement
        ↓
Update Subscription BLoC
        ↓
Show success
        ↓
Navigate/update app access
```

Do not mark a subscription as active just because the purchase button was clicked.

Only update entitlement after the purchase has been successfully completed and verified according to the existing backend/store flow.

* Purchased
* Restored
* Pending
* Canceled
* Error
* Already purchased
* Store errors
* Duplicate purchase updates
