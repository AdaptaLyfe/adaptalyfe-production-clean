Fix the **Google Play subscription** functionality in the existing Flutter application.

### Current Issue

Google subscription is currently **not working**.

Do not rebuild the subscription system from scratch. First inspect the existing implementation and identify exactly why the Google Play subscription purchase flow is failing.

## 1. Inspect the Existing Implementation

First inspect:

* `pubspec.yaml`
* Subscription service/repository
* Subscription BLoC/Cubit/provider
* Subscription screen
* Android configuration
* Google Play Billing implementation
* Product IDs
* Purchase stream/listener
* Purchase verification logic
* Restore purchase logic

* `in_app_purchase`
* `in_app_purchase_android`
* RevenueCat
* Google Play Billing directly
* Another subscription package

## 2. Google Play Product Configuration

Verify that the Android subscription product IDs in Flutter exactly match the IDs configured in Google Play Console.

Check:

* Product ID
* Subscription ID

## 3. Google Play Billing Flow

Make sure the complete flow works:

```text
Open Subscription Screen
        ↓
Load Google Play Products
        ↓
Product Found
        ↓
Display Subscription
        ↓
User Taps Subscribe
        ↓
Start Google Play Purchase
        ↓
Purchase Stream Receives Update
        ↓
Check Purchase Status
        ↓
Verify Purchase
        ↓
Update User Subscription
        ↓
Complete Purchase
        ↓
Refresh Subscription UI
```

Handle all important purchase states:

* `pending`
* `purchased`
* `restored`
* `error`
* `canceled`
* `unavailable`

Do not treat `pending` or `canceled` as successful subscriptions.

## 4. Purchase Listener

Verify that the purchase update listener is registered correctly and is not being created multiple times.

The listener should:

* Receive purchase updates.
* Process each purchase safely.
* Handle restored purchases.
* Handle errors.
* Update BLoC/state correctly.
* Complete the purchase when required.
* Avoid duplicate processing.

Make sure the listener is initialized before starting the purchase flow.

Also make sure it is properly disposed when the service/BLoC is destroyed.
